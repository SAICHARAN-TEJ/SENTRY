# Brag Plan: SUBPIXEL-SENTRY

## What is this app?
A tactical land-cover intelligence tool that super-resolves Sentinel-2 imagery from 10m to 2.5m and mathematically validates every pixel before use.

## The angle
Military-grade precision and absolute distrust of AI. We don't just generate higher resolution; we mathematically interrogate it.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A dramatic, sweeping shot over the 3D Himalayan tactical DEM, with the stark, uppercase title "TRUST, BUT VERIFY." fading in. 

## Key moments (the middle)
- The raw 10m pixelated imagery sliding away to reveal the razor-sharp 2.5m reconstruction.
- The system rejecting a false positive with a glaring red "CRITICAL: Target signature matched" alert.
- The evidence dossier appearing, showing the math that proves the AI's claim.

## Outro / punchline
The system locks down. "Find the unseen. Prove it's real." Logo out.

## User flow worth showing
Select a staged L2A scene → Run the reconstruction model → View the split-slider result and validation dossier.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: Tactical military intelligence film, high-stakes and serious.
- Interpretation: Wide shots, big typography, dramatic reveals, and restrained but impactful motion.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 23

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #000000
- Accent: #d32f2f
- Text: #f0f0fa
- Display font: Barlow
- Body font: IBM Plex Mono
- Strongest visual element: The 3D tactical DEM and the monochrome, instrument-grade panels.

## Share copy (draft)
Super-resolve satellite imagery from 10m to 2.5m/px—and mathematically prove every pixel. Trust, but verify the AI with SUBPIXEL-SENTRY.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support
- Music: cinematic
- Music treatment: Starts low, swells during the reconstruction reveal, cuts sharp on the red alert.
- Music cue guidance: To be detected at composition time. Target cues for the split-slider reveal and the red alert.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; use music bass to make the terrain pulse slightly on the beat.
- SFX posture: moderate; heavy, mechanical clicks for UI interactions, a sharp alarm for the alert.
- Audio-coupled moments: The text "TRUST, BUT VERIFY." slams in on a strong beat. 
- Restraint rule: No playful or bouncy sounds.
